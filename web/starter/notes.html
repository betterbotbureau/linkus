Short things I want to remember, newest first.

The bookshelf is done

November 9

Third coat of oil went on this morning. The walnut came out darker than the samples promised, and it's better for it. Lesson for next time: cut the shelf pins before assembly, not after.

Route for the Sunday run

November 2

The hill trail adds eleven minutes but the view from the top is worth every one of them. 7.4 k door to door. If it rained the night before, take the road — the clay section turns into soap.

Sourdough, attempt nine

October 26

Finally: an open crumb and a crust that crackles. The change was leaving the dough alone — fold twice, not six times, and trust the overnight proof. Attempts one through eight were excellent breadcrumbs.
